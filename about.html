About Me

Hi, I’m Lee Mifsud.

I am an interactive media student with an interest in UI/UX design, web development, game design, and creating digital experiences that are clear, engaging, and user-friendly.

Throughout my work, I enjoy combining design and development to build projects that are both visually appealing and functional. My projects focus on layout, usability, interaction, accessibility, and creating polished experiences for different types of users.

I am always looking to improve my skills, experiment with new ideas, and create work that shows both creativity and technical development.
